# CENTOLINHO

### Independent developer · Game creator · Worldbuilder
**Italy**

> I don't just want to build software. I want to build worlds people can get lost in.

I'm an independent, self-driven creator from Italy. I came into development through roleplay communities, where I became fascinated by the systems, environments and stories that make virtual worlds feel alive.

Since then, I've been learning by building: scripting custom gameplay systems, designing environments, mapping spaces, shaping visual identities and turning ideas into playable experiences.

I work across development and creative direction because I enjoy seeing a project as a whole, not just the code, but the world that code makes possible.

---

## What I'm building

### THE 40TH PARALLEL
**Original narrative-driven game · Unity · In development**

A dystopian story set in 2071, where people can be erased without being killed.

At the centre is Ryu Seon-gi, a former government archivist who helped erase other people's identities, until he discovers his own name on a deletion list. Inside Kyon-Zhi, an underground prison built around human exploitation, he must survive, confront his past and reconstruct a register of people the world has chosen to forget.

I'm developing the project's world, narrative foundations, gameplay direction and visual identity.

*There is no freedom below the line.*

### DETROIT 313
**Founder · FiveM · QBOX**

An Italian roleplay project inspired by Detroit, Michigan, reimagined inside Los Santos.

Detroit 313 is an attempt to build a coherent city identity rather than simply change a map: custom environments, street layouts, locations, gameplay systems and a serious roleplay philosophy where things are earned through play.

A long-running hands-on project combining mapping, scripting, visual work and community building.

## How I approach making things

I'm practical, curious and comfortable learning by doing. I like understanding how systems work, taking things apart and rebuilding them until they match the idea I had in mind.

I use modern tools, including AI-assisted tools when useful, but I don't want tools to become the author. Creative direction, decisions and responsibility should remain human.

I would rather ship something with a clear identity than produce something polished-looking but empty.

## Outside development

I'm interested in games, architecture, urban spaces, music, visual culture and the details that give places their character.

I have a particular appreciation for gritty environments, strong atmosphere and worlds that feel as though they existed before the player arrived.

## Projects

### HOMO POLITICUS
**1717 Interactive · Project 002**

*Political Grand Strategy & Life Simulation*

**Status:** Active development  
**Technology:** Unity 6 · C# · SQLite · Custom Simulation Core

An ambitious political life simulator exploring the relationship between individual ambition, society, institutions and power.

Rather than beginning as an established political leader, players start as ordinary citizens navigating education, employment, relationships, personal finances and the opportunities that may eventually lead them into political life.

The long-term vision encompasses:

- Deep personal and political career simulation.
- Dynamic societies and autonomous characters.
- Economic, institutional and governmental systems.
- Political parties, elections and international relations.
- Constitutional frameworks and different forms of government.
- Persistent consequences and individual historical legacies.
- Deterministic, modular simulation architecture.
- Cinematic grand strategy art direction.

The project is being developed around a dedicated simulation core, structured development milestones, automated testing and a clear separation between simulation logic and presentation.

*Power is temporary. Legacy is forever.*

### THE 40TH PARALLEL
**1717 Interactive · Project 001**

*Original Narrative-Driven Game*

**Status:** Development temporarily paused  
**Technology:** Unity 6 · C#

A dystopian story set in 2071, where people can be erased without being killed.

At its centre is Ryu Seon-gi, a former government archivist who helped erase other people's identities, until he discovers his own name on a deletion list.

Inside Kyon-Zhi, an underground prison built around human exploitation, he must survive, confront his past and reconstruct a register of people the world has chosen to forget.

The project explores identity, institutional oppression, memory and the consequences of absolute authority through environmental storytelling and narrative-driven gameplay.

*There is no freedom below the line.*

## Development Philosophy

I believe that ambitious projects require more than ambitious ideas.

They require structure, consistency, accountability and the willingness to rebuild things that do not meet the intended standard.

I value:

- Substance over appearance.
- Working systems over empty promises.
- Clear documentation over unnecessary complexity.
- Verified results over assumptions.
- Original creative identity over generic design.
- Sustainable development over rushed releases.

My approach combines creative ambition with structured execution. Every project should have a clear purpose, a recognizable identity and a solid foundation upon which it can evolve.

I believe that meaningful progress comes from curiosity, discipline and the ability to turn complex ideas into functional experiences.
